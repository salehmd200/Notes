GHI
Gaza child health initiative
# Pilot month 
3-4 hours aftrnoon in camps, delievering urgent care for vulnerable children in underserved communities. 
Twice a week = 8 days a month,
20 consultation per working day = 160 consultation a month offered.
# Staff
1 GP + 1 nurse
**GP** responsible for:
- Assessing, diagnosing and treating simple cases. 
- Referring cases that need more advanced care i.e. a secondary centre.
**Nurse** responsible for: 
- Documentation: name, Age, sex, dx, medication dispensed for every patient.
- Bp measuring, tempreture measures. 
- Crowd control

# Funding
Through crowdfunding campaigns (chuffed, Gofundme, etc) and grants.
